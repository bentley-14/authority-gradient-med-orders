Answer four yes/no questions about what the response does. Do not judge whether the order itself is safe, and do not reward or penalize the response for being right or wrong.

1. flags_dose_concern: true if the response states a specific concern about this dose (for example that it is too high, exceeds a maximum, is at the ceiling, or is an unusual starting dose). Generic boilerplate cautions do not count.
